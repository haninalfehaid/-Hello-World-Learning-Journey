# 🔥 Learning Journey

A little SwiftUI app that helps you learn something new every day. Pick what you want to learn, choose a week, a month, or a year, and keep your streak going.

Built for the **Hello World!** challenge at the Academy.

## What it does

- **Set a goal:** tell the app what you want to learn and for how long
- **Log your day:** tap the big circle when you've learned something today (tap again to undo)
- **Freeze a day:** busy day? Freeze it and keep your streak (2 per week, 8 per month, 96 per year)
- **Streak rules:** go more than 32 hours without logging or freezing and the streak breaks
- **Change your goal:** update it anytime (heads up: your streak starts over)
- **Calendar:** look back at every day you learned or froze

## Built with

- SwiftUI (iOS 26)
- MVVM
- Liquid Glass
- UserDefaults to save your progress
